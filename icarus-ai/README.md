# Icarus × AI: prep for the conversation with Robby

This is prep for advising Robby Smukler (founder/CEO of [Icarus Football](https://icarusfc.com/)) on where AI fits, especially in the kit design process.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`brief.md`](brief.md) | The main doc: what Icarus does today, what the best players do, the opportunities, the tool stack, a phased plan, and how to run the conversation |
| [`research-notes.md`](research-notes.md) | Raw findings with source links, for fact-checking or going deeper |

**The one-line pitch:** Use AI to turn "fill out a form and wait for a designer" into "see your kit in 60 seconds, share it, and then have an Icarus designer make it real." Don't replace the designers. Put AI in front of them as the hook, and behind them as a way to work faster.
