## Icarus Football
- Founded 2017 by Robby Smukler (Founder, CEO, and Creative Director); first sale March 2018; went full-time in 2019. Started in his bedroom, then a Kensington (Philly) warehouse, then a London office (around 2022).
- Scale claims vary: 600+ clubs (Wikipedia, Substack); 2,000+ clubs in 25 countries (about page); about 300k jerseys, about 10k unique designs, about 4,000 clients (Billy Penn, July 2024).
- Process: intake form, then a designer reaches out; design is included in the price; up to 4 free design rounds; a $50 fee to re-engage if you previously ghosted after getting designs.
- Club Shop jerseys are about $50 (special collars and long sleeves extra). One product page says made-to-order items ship 7–9 weeks after ordering.
- Icarus Cup: a 7v7 soccer, fashion, and culture festival co-run with CASA in Philly since 2021, also in Austin since 2023.
- Other clients: Grassroots Soccer (non-profit), The Town FC (2023/24), company retreats, bachelor parties.
- Sources: [About](https://icarusfc.com/pages/about) · [FAQ](https://icarusfc.com/pages/faq) · [What is a club shop](https://icarusfc.com/blogs/icarus-club-shops/what-is-a-club-shop) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icarus_FC) · [Urban Pitch profile](https://urbanpitch.com/icarus-fc/) · [Soccer Stories: Kit-industrial Complex pt 2](https://soccerstories.substack.com/p/the-kit-industrial-complex-part-2) · [Billy Penn 2024](https://billypenn.com/2024/07/09/icarus-football-soccer-brand-kensington-tournament-casa/) · [WHYY Icarus Cup](https://whyy.org/articles/philadelphia-2023-icarus-cup-casa-soccer-festival-fashion) · [Protagonist: Icarus Cup Texas](https://protagonistsoccer.com/coverage/icarus-cup-philadelphia-soccer-casa-austin-texas) · [The Town FC](https://thetownfc.com/the-town-fc-partners-with-independent-club-design-powerhouse-icarus-football-to-deliver-2023-24-kits/) · [Kicks to the Pitch](https://kickstothepitch.com/custom-soccer-kits-w-icarus-fc/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robbysmukler/) · [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/icarus-football)

## Industry examples
- **PUMA AI Creator × Man City:** 180k+ submissions; experts shortlisted 10 designs for a public fan vote; the winner was worn as the third kit. **× Marseille:** 35k users in 2 weeks, 8+ min/session, about 5 designs per user, about 1M ratings; rating others' designs earns credits. (Sources disagree on dates.) [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/puma-is-fueling-engagement-and-community-feedback-with-an-ai-platform-centered-on-co-creation/) · [Dezeen](https://dezeen.com/2026/09/01/manchester-city-ai-kit-puma) · [Inside World Football](https://insideworldfootball.com/?p=158767) · [Man City](https://www.mancity.com/citytv/mens/puma-ai-creator-design-kit-63869292)

A consumer GenAI Campus personalization campaign reported +12% e-commerce sales and 60% journey completion. ## Tools
- **Recraft:** Raster and vector seamless patterns; exports PNG, JPEG, CMYK TIFF, SVG, PDF. [Docs](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-studio/styles/seamless-patterns.md)
- **Adobe Firefly:** A 2024 user thread reported weak seamless-pattern results (may be outdated). [Thread](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-405/text-to-image-make-a-seamless-repeating-pattern-1480824)
- **PatternedAI** (textile patterns): [site](https://patterned.ai/)
- **FASHN:** On-model images and try-on, API, about $19–99/mo; open-sourced VTON v1.5 in Jan 2026. **Botika:** Turns packshots into on-model images, from about $33/mo. [Claid guide](https://claid.ai/blog/article/virtual-try-on-tools) · [FASHN](https://fashn.ai/research) · [Botika](https://botika.com/resources/thethread/best-ai-tools-on-model-product-photography)
- **Shopify 3D configurator apps:** Mirrorsize ($199–499/mo tiers); Zakeke and Kickflip (no-code). [Mirrorsize](https://apps.shopify.com/3d-configurator)
- **Canva / Printful:** Canva reportedly integrates with POD (print-on-demand) providers. No published conversion data was found. [Style3D](https://www.style3d.com/blog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/13608) · [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/au/blog/ai-and-custom-apparel)
